Know More About Me

I'm Anshul Nagaonkar, an aspiring Pilot and Aerospace Engineer.

I am currently a senior high school student, and Aerospace/Aviation has always been more than an interest to me. It's a world of engineering which includes Physics and Mathematics, Flight Operations, Precision, Teamwork, Constant Learning and the Technology.

I spend my free time exploring Aerospace and Aviation, preparing for my future Ab Initio Cadet Pilot Selections and sometimes coding and developing too. My immediate goal is to pursue a Bachelor's Degree in Aerospace Engineering and pursue my dream job as an aviator.

Beyond Aviation, I am proficient in computer programming. My core competencies include software development using Java and HTML/CSS, alongside a strong foundational understanding of Python and JavaScript. I value clean, well-structured and organised programming.

I built this portfolio to document my journey from student enthusiast to a skilled professional in the skies.
